# kika fyzio · brand files

All files are outlined vectors (no font dependency). Typeface: Plus Jakarta Sans 700 (wordmark) / 800 (mark).

- `wordmark.svg` – ink + terracotta, for light backgrounds
- `wordmark-on-dark.svg` – white + terracotta, for dark backgrounds
- `wordmark-mono.svg` – single colour (print, embossing, stamps)
- `mark.svg` – terracotta square with white "k" (favicon, social avatars, app icon), 512 px
- `mark-ink.svg` – ink square with terracotta "k"

Colours: ink `#151515`, terracotta `#C8583F`, white `#FFFFFF`.
Clear space: keep at least the height of the "k" stem free around the wordmark. Minimum wordmark height: 20 px.
